## Tide+ 1.1.1 - 10/26/26

Install the same way: the jar in `mods/` on the server and every client (replaces 1.1.0).

## Rig balance
The pieces of a rig already have their own perks for its place or time, so the rig's own bonus on top was too much. Rigs are toned down to a topping:
- **Place and time rigs at Tier 2** (Ocean, Storm, Jungle, Cave, Fire): +1 luck (+15% rare finds) and +30% catch quality, was +2 luck and +50%.
- **Swamp, Desert and Void at Tier 2**: +25% bite speed, +1 luck and +20% catch quality (was +2 luck and +36%).
- **River** +20% quality (was +32%), **Night** +1 luck and +25% quality (was +2 and +42%), **Frost** +25% quality (was +42%), **Twilight** +30% quality (was +42%).
- **Moonlight rig at Tier 2**: +1 luck (was +2), still with +25% catch quality toward a full moon.
- **Lucky rig at Tier 2**: +1 luck and +10% catch quality (was +2 luck).
- **Storm rig in a thunderstorm** stacked the most: the copper line and copper bobber no longer add luck in a thunderstorm, and the storm lure adds +1 luck there (was +2).

## Every piece's place or time matches its rig
Conditions only one or two pieces had (forest, new moon, mushroom fields, beach) are folded into the rig each piece belongs to:
- **Grove rod**: +15% rare finds in jungles (was forests), and it joins the Jungle rig.
- **Spider line** (Cave rig): +10% bite speed and +10% hook window underground (was a hook window at night and luck on a new moon).
- **Ink lure** (Night rig): +10% bite speed and +10% hook window at night, and it attracts rare fish (x1.5) at night (was x2 on a new moon only).
- **Echo bobber** (Cave rig): attracts rare fish underground (was on a new moon).
- **Mushroom bobber** (Moonlight rig): +10% bite speed at night (was in mushroom fields).
- **Slime line** (Swamp rig): its perks work in swamps (was underground).
- **Honey line** (Honey rig, works anywhere): +5% bite speed everywhere (was +15% in forests only).
- **Turtle bobber** and **squid lure** (Ocean rig): their beach perks work in oceans.
- **Pearl bobber** (Moonlight rig): +15% rare finds and +15% catch quality on a full moon (the rare finds were on beaches).
- **Golden line and golden hook** (Gold rig): no longer have luck in deserts, a leftover from the Desert rig.

## Stone is the starter rig
- **Stone rig**: the stone rod, plain fishing line, stone hook, plain (dyeable) bobber and plain bait. Tier 1: +10% bite speed and the rod wears half as fast. Tier 2: +20% bite speed, +10% fishing XP and the rod barely wears.

## Twilight lasts longer
- **Dawn and dusk** for tackle perks and the Twilight rig now last about 3 minutes each (was about 2). Which fish bite at which time is unchanged.

## The Angling Table
- **The panel is wood now**, not grey, on both tabs and on the tackle box's Assemble screen (the frame used to sit under the screen's dimmed background).
- **Using the table always opens the 3x3 crafting grid**; the Assemble tab is one button away.
- **A stats panel on the right of the Assemble screen** shows the rod being put together, rod, tackle and rigs added up, with every place, time and rig condition it cares about met. It updates as pieces go on.
- **Rig cards show up to 6 rigs**: a full card for each rig that is working, and a one-line header for the rest (a rig with one piece, or one past the two a rod carries). Each header has two boxes, Tier 1 and Tier 2, that fill in as the rig reaches them. Hover any card or header to see both bonuses and what could still go on.

## Rods
- **A rod's tooltip shows its totals**: with tackle on it, the rod lists what it all adds up to, always-on and then each place or time (a bamboo rod, vine line and apple bobber: +50% bite speed in jungles). It used to list only the rod's own perks, though the pieces always counted.
- **Rod icons are plain again**: the small rig gems are gone.
- **The iron rig's attack shows like a sword's**: "When in Main Hand", then the attack damage and attack speed in green (4 and 1.6 at Tier 1, 6 and 1.6 at Tier 2).

## Server records
- **A single low gong** for a new server record, with no sparkles around the player.

## Tide+ 1.1.0 - 10/26/26

Install the same way: the jar in `mods/` on the server and every client (replaces 1.0.20).

## Material rigs
New always-on rigs that work anywhere (2 pieces give the small bonus, 3 or more the full one), so the basic tackle has a role:
- **Stone rig** (stone rod, stone hook, plain line): the rod wears half as fast with two pieces, and barely at all with three.
- **Gold rig** (golden rod or Midas rod, golden line, golden hook, golden spoon lure): +10% fishing XP; with three, +20% XP and a 25% chance of a gold nugget with each fish.
- **Diamond rig** (diamond rod, line, hook, bobber): the catch-quality rig. +10% catch quality, +25% with three. The diamond bobber now gives catch quality (+8%) instead of bite speed.
- **Honey rig** (honeycomb rod, honey line, honey bobber): a 15% chance of a honeycomb with each fish; with three, a 25% chance of a honey bottle and an easier landing.
- Iron (the sword), Copper (Storm rig) and Netherite (Fire rig) are unchanged.
- **Lucky rig** (golden apple bobber, enchanted golden apple bobber, lucky hook, magnet lure, lucky bait): +15% rare finds, +30% with three.

## Sunlight and Moonlight rigs
- **Sunlight rig** (sunflower rod, spinner lure, golden bobber), by day: bite speed that follows the sun (up to +20% at Tier 1, +40% at Tier 2, strongest at noon, nothing at dawn and dusk), and **seeds** come up with fish: wheat and beetroot at Tier 1, plus pumpkin and melon at Tier 2.
- **Moonlight rig** (amethyst rod, line, hook and bobber, pearl bobber, mushroom bobber, moon bait), at night: rare finds, and at Tier 2 catch quality too, strongest on a full moon and fading to nothing on a new moon. It also brings up an amethyst shard 10% of the time at Tier 1, 20% at Tier 2.
- **Amethyst moved to the moon**: amethyst catches moonlight now. Each piece gives catch quality at night that grows toward a full moon, and the pieces count toward the Moonlight rig. (Its sunlight bite speed went to the Sunlight rig.)

## Rigs are easier to read
- **Tier 1 and Tier 2**: every rig now says Tier 1 (2+) and Tier 2 (3+) on tooltips, the recipe book, the rod panel and the Angling Table.
- **Every piece belongs to one rig at most.** Moved: moon bait (Moonlight rig only), slime line (Swamp rig only), golden line and golden hook (Gold rig only), golden bobber (Sunlight rig only), honey bobber (Honey rig only); the old Amethyst set became part of the Moonlight rig. New homes: the spider line joins the Cave rig (the Night rig keeps the phantom line), and the redstone line joins the Desert rig.
- **A rod carries two rigs at most**: if it has pieces of three (bait counts too), the two with the most pieces count and the third says "not counted".
- **Each rig has its own colour**, used for its name, its card at the table and its gem.
- **Rods show their rigs on the icon**: a small gem in the corner of the rod, in the rig's colour, one for each rig it carries (bright for Tier 2, dimmer for Tier 1). A rod picks up its gems the next time you change anything on it.

## The Angling Table and tackle box look the part
- **The table has a wooden frame with brass corners** (both tabs), each empty slot shows a faded picture of what goes there with a small label (Rod, Line, Bobber, Hook, Lure), and the rig cards beside it are one card per rig: its colour, its tier, dots for the pieces it has, what it gives now and what the next piece adds.
- **The tackle box has a carrying handle and tray dividers.**

## Cut bait and chum
- **Cut bait remembers the fish it was cut from**, and better fish make better bait: with it on the rod, at least this share of bites are rare, very rare or legendary (where rare fish live at all): Poor 15%, Average 20%, Good 30%, Great 45%, Trophy 75%, Mythic every time. Cut it at the Angling Table from a fish and flint (4 per fish).
- **Chum the water**: when a hook with chum on the rod settles, the water around it is chummed for 2 minutes, and **everyone** fishing within 12 blocks bites 25% faster.

## Tackle with a trick
- **Fish bone hook takes the bone hook's place** (its stats, its Desert rig spot, and it is a Tier 1 hook). The bone hook is no longer made; an old one still works on a rod and can be turned into a fish bone hook at the table.
- **Stone hook** sinks to the bottom: a 15% chance of kelp and 10% of seagrass with each fish.
- **Emerald hook**: +10% fishing XP and a 3% chance of an emerald with each fish.
- **Barbed hook** lands almost anything (Good and Bad hooks always, even a Terrible one half the time), but it tears the fish: never better than Great, and half the time a tier worse. The card says when it did.
- **Heart bobber** heals you three hearts with each fish.
- **Lichen bobber** glows (lights the water like the glow hook), bites 20% faster underground, and joins the Cave rig.
- **Village rod** teaches: players fishing within 16 blocks of you get +10% fishing XP.
- **Crystal rod** tells you what bit, how good it is and how rare, the moment it bites (handy for a fish you keep losing), and gives +10% hook window and +10% to land Good and Bad hooks.

## Levels and rare fish
- **A high fishing level no longer makes rare fish easy.** Levels still widen the hook zones and calm common and uncommon fish, but rare, very rare and legendary fish fight at least as hard, with hook zones no wider, than in original Tide, however high your level. Low levels are still harder on them. To take on a hard fish, use tackle: a wider hook window, an easier fight, a landing hook, or the barbed hook to land it at the cost of its quality.

## Coal line merged
- **The coal line is retired**; the redstone line took in its hook window (+5%). An old coal line still works on a rod and turns back into a fishing line at the table.

## Crates
- **Crates may hold string (2-5) or a fishing line** (35% of crates), to help with crafting.
- **Basic tackle turns up more, the best tackle very rarely.** Fishing crates hold tackle a little more often (30%, was 15%), now including plain lines and hooks, stone and iron pieces; golden pieces are rare, and a diamond hook, line or bobber (or netherite in Nether crates) is about 1 crate in 200. The fishing boat chest has a few basics too.

## Rod panel
- **Shows only what your rod changes.** A stat the rod, its tackle, your gear and your level leave alone is not listed.
- **Rigs and conditions at a glance.** Under the stats: the rigs the rod carries (with their tier), in the rig's colour when they are working here and now and grey when not; then every time, place and weather the rod's own pieces care about (night, rain, oceans...), bright when it applies and grey when it doesn't. The Sunlight and Moonlight rigs, amethyst and the wayfinder rod show how strong they are right now as a percentage.

## Tide+ 1.0.20 - 10/26/26

Install the same way: the jar in `mods/` on the server and every client (replaces 1.0.19).

## Rod panel
- **Every stat is always shown** (no more sneaking): cast range, bite speed, hook window, rare finds, catch quality, fishing XP, land Good/Bad, fish fight, catch difficulty, second fish and comes up cooked. A dash means the gear gives none of that, so two rods are easy to compare. Enchantments get no rows of their own.
- **Lure folds into bite speed.** The Lure enchantment, bait and a rod's own speed count as bite speed, added to the bite speed from your tackle, rigs and level. One number, +% like everything else.

## Bite speed
- **One stacked number with diminishing returns** (no hard cap). Each point of Lure/bait speed is worth +6% (it used to cut the wait by a lot more). Bite speed counts in full up to +50%, half as much from +50% to +100%, and a quarter as much past that, so the fastest possible rod is still well short of instant and most of the speed comes from your tackle.

## Catch quality
- **"Quality bump" is gone.** Gear that had a chance to raise the catch a tier (amethyst rod and bobber, barbed hook, golden bait, and every full rig) now gives more catch quality instead (about 60% of the old bump chance, added to its quality). Mythic stays as rare as it was meant to be.
- **A perfect hook can still raise a fish a tier, but never to Mythic** (Trophy is the top).

## Amethyst is a daytime set
- **The amethyst rod, hook, line and bobber are strongest by day** (crystals catch the light) and only a little useful at night. By day a full amethyst set gives more catch quality than before; at night about a fifth as much. Place-and-time rigs still beat it where they apply.

## Luck is now "Rare finds"
- **Luck shows as a percentage**: "Rare finds +15%" per point, on the rod panel, item tooltips and the angler page (the old "+1 luck" is the same thing). It makes the rare fish (the ones with a luck bonus) more likely by that share of their own chance, so every rare fish benefits the same.
- **Crates are less luck-driven**: luck now adds +4% a point to the crate chance, up to +40% (it was +10% a point, up to +100%).
- The journal's luck requirement (the Midas fish) is shown as a percentage too.

## Fish sizes overlap
- **Each quality is its own bell curve of sizes**, centred higher for better fish but overlapping: a Mythic can be a small fish, and an Average one can, rarely, be a big one. Sizes keep growing slowly past the old top instead of stopping at a hard cap, so there is no size that can never be beaten. For a trout-shaped fish (typically 30-60 cm): an Average one is usually 0.7-4 lb, a Mythic usually 4.6-23 lb, about 13 lb at the median.
- **The catch line in chat shows how your fish compares with the server record** when you hover it.

## Iron rig
- **Hitting mobs wears the rod down** (1 durability per hit, like a sword). A rod worn out by other things stops at broken until repaired, but:
- **An iron-rig rod at its last point of durability breaks for real** when it hits something (its line, hook, bobber and lure drop).

## Rare finds and bite speed both soften
- **Rare finds use the same diminishing returns as bite speed**: full value up to +50%, half from +50% to +100%, a quarter past that. "+150%" never means a guaranteed rare fish: it means each rare fish is that much more likely than its own normal chance (a 2-in-100 fish at +100% is about 4-in-100).
- **Every luck and speed number is a percentage now**: bait tooltips ("+30% rare finds", "+12% bite speed"), rod tooltips, `/fishinglevel` and the angler page. Nothing says "luck" or "fishing speed" points any more.

## Balance
- **Pearl bobber**: +10% catch quality (was +20%; it costs one ender pearl). Its full-moon quality and beach rare finds are unchanged.
- **Diamond hook**: +15% hook window, +5% catch quality, +5% landing (was +12% window, +5% landing), so it is worth more than the circle hook for anything but landing.
- **Circle hook**: +5% hook window (was +10%); still +40% to land Good and Bad hooks and an easier fight.
- **Diamond bobber**: +15% bite speed (was +12%). **Lichen bobber**: +6% (was +10%; glow lichen is easy to find).
- **Cherry rod**: +40% bite speed at dawn and dusk (was +60%).
- **Wayfinder rod grows with distance**: within 500 blocks of world spawn it is a sturdy all-rounder (cast range, hook window, an easier fight, landing). From there out it gains catch quality, rare finds and fishing XP, reaching +15% quality, +30% rare finds and +10% XP at 5,000 blocks (a Nether block counts as 8). Close to home it is weaker than before; far away, a little stronger.

## The Angling Table is the fishing workbench
- **All fishing gear is made at the Angling Table**: rods, lines, bobbers, hooks, lures, bait and gear (journal, tackle box, satchel, gauges, waders...). The crafting table and smithing table no longer make them. The vanilla fishing rod, the Angling Table itself, fish-to-resource recipes and line dyeing stay at the crafting table.
- **Craft tab: a 3x3 grid**, like a crafting table. Rod upgrades (iron, gold, diamond, crystal...) go in the grid too: the rod, the upgrade and its extra, in any arrangement. The rod keeps its enchantments and parts.
- **A fishing recipe book** beside the grid, with filters for Rods, Lines, Bobbers, Hooks, Lures, Bait, Gear and Rigs (pick a rig to see its pieces). Click a recipe to move its items into the grid (shift-click for as many as you can make). If you're short, the grid shows what it takes. There's a "can make" filter and a button to hide the book.
- **Recipes are discovered** from their materials, like vanilla's recipe book: bamboo shows the bamboo rod, redstone and a fishing line show the redstone line, a Tier 1 hook and a diamond show the diamond hook. Sticks, string and planks don't count, so string alone doesn't show every line. A popup says when you discover one. Undiscovered rig pieces show as "?". You can still make a recipe you know by hand.
- **Assemble tab**: the old rod screen (line, bobber, hook, lure), one click away. The table opens on whichever tab you used last.

## Changes
- **Fishing levels**: from level 30 on, each level's cost climbs half as fast (level 99 is about 40% less XP than before). Everyone keeps their XP, so anyone past 30 goes up a few levels.
- **Hooks are labelled Tier 1 or Tier 2.** Tier 2 hooks are made from a Tier 1 hook and keep its perks.
- **Rods don't break any more.** A worn-out rod stops at broken and won't cast until it's repaired, with string at an anvil (or two of the same rod, or Mending). Its line, hook, bobber, lure and enchantments stay on. This works for every Tide rod.
- **The catch card** shows the XP and where it came from (`+44 fishing XP (10 fish, +5 Good, +20 new species, +6 perfect hook, +3 tackle)`). The level shows only on a level-up (it's on the rod panel).
- **Weights**: fish under a pound show in ounces ("0.16 oz", "5.3 oz"). A record has to beat the old one by enough to show on the scale; a tie keeps the old holder. "Your record" goes by weight too.
- **Length is no longer shown** (weight is what counts). A mounted fish's size on the wall now follows its weight.
- **Tooltips wrap**: long lines (rig bonuses, perks) wrap instead of running across the screen.

## Tide+ 1.0.0 - 10/23/26

The first full release of **Tide+**, a Fabric fork of [Tide 2](https://github.com/Lightning-64/Tide-2) (2.1.1) by Lightning64 for **Minecraft 26.3**. Everything Tide does is still here; Tide+ adds a fishing level, catch quality, and a whole tackle system on top.

**Install:** put the jar in `mods/` on the server and every player's client, with Fabric Loader 0.19.5+ and Fabric API for 26.3. It replaces Tide (same mod ID, so worlds and items carry over); don't install both.

## Fishing levels
- Levels 1 to 99 from fishing XP, with a new title every 10 levels. Level raises cast distance, bite speed, hook zones, luck and makes fish fight a bit easier; level 40 fishes like original Tide.
- A new title gets the big on-screen announcement and a firework; ordinary level-ups show on the catch card. Level-ups are announced in chat.
- `/fishinglevel get | set | addxp | reset [everything] | fair` for ops. *Fair play* puts everyone at level 40 for contests.

## Catches
- **Catch quality**: Poor, Average, Fine, Prime, Trophy, Mythic, decided at the bite. Better fish are bigger, heavier and fight harder; Trophy and Mythic catches are announced to everyone. Fish from crates get a quality too.
- **Hook zones** in the minigame: perfect, great, good, bad, miss, each with its own chance to land the fish. A perfect hook gives extra XP.
- Every fish has a length and a weight (US units by default, `useImperial` in the client config).
- **Catch card** above the hotbar for five seconds: the fish in its quality colour, size, anything special (new species, new record, two on the hook, perfect hook) and anything else that came up with it, plus the XP. Chat keeps just the XP line.
- **Favorite lures**: every fish has one, and it's twice as likely to bite on it. The lure's tooltip names the ones you've caught; the rest you find by fishing. Each cast tells you how many fish there favor your lure.
- The journal records best quality, heaviest, longest and shortest of each fish, and shows each fish's favorite lure.

## Tackle
Rods hold a line, hook, bobber and lure, plus bait. Each piece does something, and many work best in a place, time or weather (shown in purple).
- **31 rods**: Tide's 13 plus a kid's rod, bamboo, bone, copper, amethyst, mangrove, cherry, frost, cactus, conduit, clockwork and wayfinder rods, and six rare **found rods**:
  - Rotten (zombies and husks): fishes up mob drops
  - Widow (spiders): strong at night, sluggish by day
  - Drowned (drowned): fishes up sunken loot
  - Hex (witches): a chance of a helpful potion effect
  - Sickle (harvested wheat): makes nearby crops grow
  - Grove (leaves): fishes up saplings, sticks and apples
- **18 lines** (any line can be dyed any colour: line + dye), **23 hooks** (upgrades made on a base hook keep its perks), **33 bobbers** (made from sticks, a plank and the item that gives them their look), **15 lures**, and **22 baits** including region baits.
- **Lava fishing**: the lavaproof hook, blazing and netherite rods, netherite line and bobber, fiery hook and magma lure all have lava perks.
- **Matched rigs**: three or more pieces of one family (ocean, river, night, twilight, storm, swamp, jungle, desert, frost, cave, fire, void) add a bonus where that family is made for.
- **Rod stats panel** at the right edge while a rod is in hand: everything your rod, tackle, gear and level add up to, and which perks are working right now.
- **Angler's gear** (bucket hat, vest, waders, wading boots) with a full-set bonus.
- Breaking a rod drops what was on it. Rods' hanging lines sway in the hand and inventory, and the glow hook lights the water around it.

## Storage
- **Tackle box**: 27 slots of tackle plus a rack for 3 rods. An *Assemble rod* button (or sneak + right-click) opens an angling table that uses the box's tackle directly.
- **Fish satchel**: right-click it in your inventory (or sneak + right-click in hand) to see all 64 places and take out the fish you want. Right-click in hand still turns fish collecting on and off.

## Journal
- A **Tackle** section: every piece of tackle, blacked out until you find it, with what it does and its recipe. Recipes appear in the recipe book once you hold a related item.
- An **Angler** page with your level, title and stats.

## For server admins
- A `[Tide catch]` line in the server log for every fish (Solstone reads it for contests and the Fishing tab).
- Settings are plain JSON in `config/tide.json`; no Cloth Config or Mod Menu needed.
- Fabric only, 26.x only; third-party compat from Tide (Stardew Fishing, Starcatcher, seasons mods) isn't included.

Licensed MPL-2.0, like Tide. Credit for Tide itself, its fish, art and mechanics goes to Lightning64.

## Tide v2.0 - 1/9/26

---
### Fishing Loot
- Fishing loot is now controlled by a custom data-driven system, consisting of the following:

**Fish Data**
- Fish catch conditions, rarities, rarity modifiers, journal data, minigame stats, display data, and more are now controlled using fish-specific JSON files located at `data/tide/fishing/fish`

**Crate Data**
- Crate catch conditions, rarities, rarity modifiers, and more are now controlled using crate-specific JSON files located at `data/tide/fishing/crates`

**Fishing Loot Data**
- External loot tables for fishing (such as the vanilla junk and treasure tables) can now be configured or added via JSON files located at `data/tide/fishing/loot`

More extensive documentation for these new data-driven formats will be available later after the full release.

---
### Fish
- Significant changes to the fish lineup have taken place. These include:
  - A few fish have been removed that either didn't fit with the mod's theme or were originally added to serve as filler content.
  - Added many new fish to discover throughout the world, bringing the total to around 100 fish in total.
- The vast majority of existing fish items were retextured (finally).
- Each fish can now be cooked and eaten, regardless of how healthy it might seem.
- Every single fish in the mod now has a corresponding entity version to go with it.
- Reworked the entity spawning system to be partially based off of the fish's Fish Data to automatically determine spawn conditions.
- Added natural spawning for each fish entity (except for a select few).
- By default, fish items can no longer be stacked up to 64 like before (see below, or look at the config tab titled "Items" for more information).

**Fish Lengths**
- Each fish you catch is now assigned a length in centimeters based on configurable parameters from the fish's Fish Data.
- Config options have been created to allow the user to disable this mechanic entirely, or show their extreme catches only in the journal and not affect item stacks.
- The fish's length will serve as an extra collectible aspect, but may affect other things (such as the quality of the fish food item if Quality Food is installed).

**Bucketable Fish**
- Each fish item you catch is now assigned a timestamp that is used to determine if the fish is "alive" or not.
- If the fish item is "alive", you can click on the fish in the inventory GUI with a water bucket (or lava bucket) in order to transform the items into a fish bucket item to allow access to the fish's entity form.
- If the fish is "alive" and bucketable, a tooltip saying "Bucketable" will be shown below the fish's name.
- Config options have been added so users can either never allow bucketable fish, allow them all the time, or change how long a fish item should be "alive" for after being caught.

---
### Fishing Journal
- Mechanics and visuals for the Fishing Journal have been reworked. Some of the major differences are listed below
- The fishing journal is now deeply integrated into the aforementioned "Fish Data" system to dynamically create profiles for any fish with a proper data file. Developers will no longer have to create separate data files for journal entries if a fish data file is defined.
- The fish "profile" GUI has also been overhauled with a fresh new layout.
- Removed the old "page" system in favor of categorizing fish more loosely. This also means that all fish, whether locked or unlocked, are visible in the journal GUI at all times.
- Additionally, many fish that were originally split up into smaller categories have been merged into corresponding larger categories.
- Fish can be assigned rarities, profile descriptions, and location summaries via the Fish Data system.
- **Simply collecting a fish item will no longer unlock its profile in the fishing journal!** Each fish you unlock _must_ be caught while fishing in order to unlock the profile.

---
### Mechanics
- The time it takes to catch a fish now also varies based on the type of liquid you're fishing in (ex. x1.0 for water, x1.6 for lava, x1.8 for void). This multiplier is applied before fishing speed modifications like bait or lure enchantments.
- Fish caught in specific biomes will be more lenient about where they are catchable. For example, if you're fishing in a river biome that cuts through a desert biome, you're still able to catch desert-themed fish.
- The temperature preferences of fish are no longer based on specific biomes. Instead, they are based on the internal temperature values from the worldgen noise router. This means that fishing in the middle of a desert may favor more warm fish than if you were to fish on the border between a desert and a more temperate biome.
- A few additional core mechanics have been added or modified:

**Minigame**
- The fishing minigame has received many tweaks, such as the following:
- The minigame no longer operates on a probability-based catch system, instead having two distinct zones on the minigame bar.
- The lighter zone in the bar will successfully catch the fish if clicked over, but the darker zone will cause the catch to fail and the minigame to end.
- Each fish has a configurable "strength" and "speed" parameter in their Fish Data that influences how much space the dark zone covers and how quickly the vertical bar moves, respectively.
- Not every fish will move the vertical bar in a "sine wave" pattern anymore, with some fish having much stranger movements. Stay alert!
- The fish's movement pattern is defined in its Fish Data as well, being set to one of the following:
  - `sine`
  - `plateau`
  - `jitter`
  - `darts`
  - `linear`
  - `linear_wrap`
- Additionally, the lighter bar will change color depending on what liquid you're fishing in (ex. blue for water, orange for lava, purple for void).

**Void Fishing**
- In order to fish in the void, you must first craft a Hook of the Depths and apply it to any fishing rod via the Angling Table.
- With the hook applied, fishing in the void operates as if the entire void is a fishable liquid. Note that the fishable void does not line up with the death barrier, and may extend significantly farther up in the world (ex. the End dimension)
- New fish have been added for both the End void and the void beneath the overworld bedrock.
- A few new particles have been added that are displayed on the fishable void surface.

### Items
- Added a few "profiling" items such as the thermometer and pocket watch, which can assist the user in finding the precise locations of fish when paired with the fishing journal or fishy notes.
- Most legendary tier fish have late-game craftable items, each filling a different role in player progression.
- Some other utility items were also added:

**Fish Satchel**
- Added the Fish Satchel, which is basically a bundle that can store fish.
- Right-clicking while holding the fish satchel will open or close the satchel. When open, any fish you catch or pick up from the ground will be automatically transferred to the fish satchel.
- This item should be especially helpful for dealing with the new unstackable fish.

**Fishy Notes**
- Pre-2.0 "Torn Notes" have been replaced by a new item, called the Fishy Note.
- Fishy Notes can be found in crates, with similar drop chances compared to the aforementioned Torn Notes.
- A Fishy Note for a specific fish can also be crafted using the desired fish and a single piece of paper.
- Each Fishy Note will display the catch conditions, rarity, and relative size of a single fish, usually one that has not been discovered yet.
- The conditions are displayed in the same manner as you would see them in the Fishing Journal, except the name and sprite of the fish are not visible.
- A checkmark will be displayed in the Fishy Note GUI if the fish it references is already discovered.

**Fish Display**
- The Fish Display is a placeable block that allows the user to mount a fish item to the wall for decoration purposes.
- Once a display is placed, right-clicking it with any fish item that has display data defined will display the entity version of the fish item on the wall. Right-clicking again will retrieve the item stack.
- Display data such as rotation and offsets are defined in the Fish Data files.

### Miscellaneous
- Fishing rods can now be repaired at an anvil using string in the same way that vanilla tools and armor are upgraded.
- Most of the old advancements have been renamed, and many new advancements have been added.
- Two new utility commands have been added, `/journal` and `/fishing`.
- Removed the old fishing boat structure, I don't think it's really necessary.
- Many item and block IDs have been changed to reflect updated English translation names. For example, the Angling Table (previously having an ID of `tide:angler_workshop`) has been renamed to `tide:angling_table`. **This may cause issues if Tide 2 is loaded on a world that was previously loaded with Tide 1!**

I won't list bug fixes here because so many things got reworked, and it's likely that many old bugs are now patched.
