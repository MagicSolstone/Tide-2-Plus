# Tide+ 1.0.19

Install the same way: the jar in `mods/` on the server and every client (replaces 1.0.18).

## Rod panel (the fishing stats on the right)
- **Short by default**: the level and XP bar, then cast range, bite speed, hook window and luck. **Sneak** to see everything else (catch quality, fish fight, extras, your enchantments, and which perks are active where you are). It says "Sneak for N more" when something is hidden.
- **One bite-speed number.** Lure, bait, the rod, your gear and your fishing level all add up into "Bite speed", shown as +% like every other row (faster is always +). The separate "Lure and bait: -% wait" row is gone. A Lure enchantment shows how much it adds when you sneak.

## Rigs
- **Iron rig now has a two-piece tier**: two pieces make the rod hit like a **wooden sword**, three like an **iron sword**. Hitting mobs never wears the rod down.
- **The Rigs filter in the Angling Table's recipe book is a Nether star** (it was a lead).

## For modders
- **CODE_GUIDE.md**: a map of the code, a "where do I change X" table, and how to add a hook, rig or setting.
