# Tide+ 1.1.1

Install the same way: the jar in `mods/` on the server and every client (replaces 1.1.0).

## Rig balance
The pieces of a rig already have their own perks for its place or time, so the rig's own bonus on top was too much. Rigs are toned down to a topping:
- **Place and time rigs at Tier 2** (Ocean, Storm, Jungle, Cave, Fire): +1 luck (+15% rare finds) and +30% catch quality, was +2 luck and +50%.
- **Swamp, Desert and Void at Tier 2**: +25% bite speed, +1 luck and +20% catch quality (was +2 luck and +36%).
- **River** +20% quality (was +32%), **Night** +1 luck and +25% quality (was +2 and +42%), **Frost** +25% quality (was +42%), **Twilight** +30% quality (was +42%).
- **Moonlight rig at Tier 2**: +1 luck (was +2), still with +25% catch quality toward a full moon.
- **Lucky rig at Tier 2**: +1 luck and +10% catch quality (was +2 luck).
- **Storm rig in a thunderstorm** stacked the most: the copper line and copper bobber no longer add luck in a thunderstorm, and the storm lure adds +1 luck there (was +2).

## Stone is the starter rig
- **Stone rig**: the stone rod, plain fishing line, stone hook, plain (dyeable) bobber and plain bait. Tier 1: +10% bite speed and the rod wears half as fast. Tier 2: +20% bite speed, +10% fishing XP and the rod barely wears.

## Twilight lasts longer
- **Dawn and dusk** for tackle perks and the Twilight rig now last about 3 minutes each (was about 2). Which fish bite at which time is unchanged.

## The Angling Table
- **The panel is wood now**, not grey, on both tabs and on the tackle box's Assemble screen (the frame used to sit under the screen's dimmed background).
- **Using the table always opens the 3x3 crafting grid**; the Assemble tab is one button away.
- **Rig cards show up to 6 rigs**: a full card for each rig that is working, and a one-line header for the rest (a rig with one piece, or one past the two a rod carries). Each header has two boxes, Tier 1 and Tier 2, that fill in as the rig reaches them. Hover any card or header to see both bonuses and what could still go on.

## Rods
- **Rod icons are plain again**: the small rig gems are gone.
- **The iron rig's attack shows like a sword's**: "When in Main Hand", then the attack damage and attack speed in green (4 and 1.6 at Tier 1, 6 and 1.6 at Tier 2).

## Server records
- **A single low gong** for a new server record, with no sparkles around the player.
