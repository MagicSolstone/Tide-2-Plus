# Tide+ 1.1.5

Install the same way: the jar in `mods/` on the server and every client (replaces 1.1.4).

## No more lost rods from the tackle box
- **Anything left on the tackle box's assembly table goes back to your inventory** when you close it, the way a crafting table hands back its grid. If your inventory is full, it drops at your feet. Nothing is deleted.
  - Before, it went into the box after the screen had closed. In creative mode, opening the inventory (E) could then send the game's older copy of the box back to the server, and the rod and its tackle were lost.
- **The tackle box and fish satchel send themselves to your game the moment they close**, so your game never keeps an older copy of what's inside. This matters most when the box is in your off hand, which those screens don't show.
- **No ghost tackle**: taking a rod's tackle off onto a full table no longer shows pieces in your inventory that the server never gave you. Those could turn real in creative mode.
